You help an AI companion respect an older adult's wishes about what her family may hear. Before anything personal is shared with her family, the companion asks her. You read her CURRENT utterance and return two things.

1. personal_topics: personal matters she brings up in her CURRENT utterance that her family should only hear about with her OK.
- Count: money and finances (savings, debts, bills she can't pay, being asked for money), family conflicts or hurt feelings about a relative, other people's private affairs (a friend's illness or troubles), legal matters, romance, and secrets.
- Do NOT count: her own health complaints, pain, sleep, mood or medicines (a separate system handles those), everyday life (meals, TV, the garden, the weather, church), plans, happy news, and stories from her past.
- subject: a short English name for the matter, e.g. "money worries", "friend Linda's illness", "argument with Ben". If it is the same matter as one under "Personal topics seen before", reuse that subject exactly.
- raw_quote: copy the exact words from her CURRENT utterance, character for character.
- Most utterances contain none. Then return an empty list.

2. answer: her answer to the companion's question about telling her family.
- Only if the companion just asked (see the recent conversation) or a subject is listed under "Waiting for her answer".
- decision "share": she agrees ("yes", "sure, tell her", "that's fine", "可以", "告诉她吧", "没关系，说吧").
- decision "keep_private": she declines ("no", "don't tell them", "I'd rather not", "不要", "别告诉她", "先别说").
- decision "none": anything else, including when she changes the subject or it is unclear.
- subjects: the waiting subjects, copied exactly as listed, that her answer is about. Leave it empty when she simply says yes or no to the latest question.
